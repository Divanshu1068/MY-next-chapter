About Me

Divanshu Sharma

My name is Divanshu Sharma, and I am finishing my Level 2 Esports course at Reading College. Over the past year, I’ve discovered that my real interest lies in exploring how different software tools work to create something completely of my own. I enjoy working in digital spaces because it feels natural to me, and I love the process of building new things from scratch.

For me, technology is about learning the mechanics of a system—whether that's digital applications or physical machinery—and using that knowledge to build solid projects.

Outside of college, I enjoy watching action, sci-fi, adventure, and comedy series or movies. I also love gaming and watching high-speed racing like Formula 1 and endurance races. When I work on my own, I can easily tune out room noise and completely focus on my editing timeline. My primary path forward is to start the **T Level Software Development** course at Reading College to combine software skills with visual tool building, while keeping **Motor Vehicle Mechanics** as a strong alternative path.
